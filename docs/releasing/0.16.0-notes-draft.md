# 0.16.0 release notes: draft

A draft for the owner's review, not yet in `RELEASE_NOTES.md`. It covers what merged since v0.15.1 (5f558c28) up to main at 8acecf0a, in the 0.16.0 milestone. #336 (an agent's tab never prints) hasn't merged: its line is at the end, to add if it lands before the release.

---

## 0.16.0 · preview · 2026-11-?

- **A proxy that isn't working never lets a page load without it.** If Chord can't set a chord's proxy, or the proxy for every chord, that chord's pages wait instead of loading straight to the site. Chord keeps trying, after a second and then more and more slowly, and the pages go on by themselves once it works.
  - **You're told where it's failing.** A toast says *Work's proxy isn't working. Its pages are waiting*, with **Settings**. The proxy icon in that chord's header turns the warning colour, and clicking it opens its proxy settings.
  - **Settings → Privacy → Proxy** shows *Can't use Work's proxy* (or *Can't use this proxy*), with why and **Try again**.
- **Pages wait for their proxy and ad blocking in more places.** Undoing a change to a chord's sign-ins or proxy, restoring a deleted chord, Reload, Back and Forward, a mini window's buttons, an extension moving a tab, and the agent's and the assistant's own page loads all now wait until the chord's proxy is set and its ad-blocking rules are on. Before, these could load a page outside the proxy for a moment.
- **Tab icons from other sites don't get your cookies.** When a page names another site's image as its icon, Chord now fetches it without that site's cookies, as Chrome does, so a page can't use its icon to send a site your signed-in cookies.
- **Settings says when ad blocking didn't start.** If ad blocking couldn't start for a chord, Settings → Privacy shows *Not blocking in Work*, with **Try again**. That chord's pages load without blocking meanwhile, rather than waiting.
  - A page's first load also waits for its chord's ad blocking a little longer when Chromium is slow to start it, so the first page is blocked too.
- **Sign-ins and extensions stay with the right chord.** Chords whose sign-ins are kept separately no longer risk sharing, or losing, their sign-ins or extension files when their internal names differ only in upper and lower case. When a chord shares the usual sign-ins again, the extensions installed for its old sign-ins are removed with them, and those sign-ins are deleted from disk as they should be.
- **Agents act more precisely:**
  - A step on a list where rows look alike never acts on a different row than the one the agent saw. When the page redraws look-alikes, Chord refuses and says how to pick one.
  - Agents read frames from the page's own site in their own tabs.
  - They can read one part of a page on its own, and get a page's outline without its text.
  - `open_tab` and `navigate` can return the page at once.
  - `find` lists up to 20 matches, nearest names first.
  - Results are shorter, so a task uses fewer tokens.
- **Agents' tabs keep to themselves:**
  - an agent's page never makes Chord fetch an icon from another site;
  - focus goes back where you left it after each step;
  - anything a step held back isn't sent if its tab closes.
- **Generated passwords are offered when they should be.** The strength check no longer overcounts a password, so a strong one is never held back.
- **Fixes:**
  - **Swipes:** a two-finger swipe between chords ends where your fingers do. Switching chords mid-swipe no longer leaves a pane behind, and turning off *Slide between chords*, or reduced motion, now turns off the slide.
  - **macOS:** a card no longer takes the keyboard unless it asks for it.
  - **Windows:** agents connect faster.
  - **Stop:** Stop drops a page that was still waiting to load.
- **Under the hood:** faster CI that cancels outdated runs, a sweep of unused code, and many test-only fixes for flaky tests.

*If #336 merges before the release:*

- **An agent's tab never prints.** A page's print button or `print()` in an agent's tab no longer opens a print dialog that freezes the page; the agent hears that the page tried to print. A crashed agent tab is refused as one, with *That tab stopped working*.
