Everything in its place

A sidebar for your tabs, a bar for everything else, and the page gets the rest of the window.

Chords keep things apart

Work, home, a trip you're planning: give each its own chord, with its own pinned tabs and its own color, and keep sign-ins separate if you like. Switch with Ctrl/⌘ + 1–9, or swipe with two fingers.

Today's tabs tidy themselves into the Archive after a while, so the sidebar stays short. Nothing is lost: it's all a search away.

One bar reaches everything

Press Ctrl/⌘ + T and type. Open tabs, pinned tabs, history, the Archive and Chord's own actions come up together, and the page you're heading to starts connecting while you type.

Pages side by side

Drag a tab onto the page to put it beside or below the one you're reading. Up to four fit, and they stay together as one row in the sidebar.

Duet, when you want a hand

A side panel for quick questions about the page or your tabs, answered by your own Claude Code or Codex app and subscription. It shows what it read and links to where it found each answer. Ask it to fill in a form, and you check each value before it goes in: you press send.

You choose what it can read, down to nothing at all. It never sees your sign-ins, and disappears entirely if you turn it off.

  • Effects

    Restyle any site your way: your own CSS, hide what’s in the way, a reading width, or ask Duet. One switch takes them off.

  • Ads and trackers blocked

    Built in, with EasyList and EasyPrivacy, checked on your computer. Turn it off for one site in a click.

  • Bring your tabs along

    Import bookmarks, history and open tabs from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox or Safari.

  • Videos that follow you

    Now playing in the sidebar controls whatever's playing, and pops a video out into picture-in-picture.

  • Screenshots built in

    An area, an element, the visible page or the whole page, copied or saved.

  • Reader view

    Just the article, at a size and width that suit you, in Onest or your computer's reading serif.

  • Chrome extensions

    Password managers and the rest, with their buttons on a shelf in the sidebar.

  • Minimal when you need room

    Hide the sidebar and Chord shrinks to one slim bar. A click or a shortcut brings it back.

  • Links in their place

    Send a site’s links to the chord it belongs in, links from other apps included.

  • Google-free, if you like

    One switch stops Chord contacting Google on its own: no Safe Browsing, Google search or dictionary downloads.

  • A proxy per chord

    One proxy for every chord, or one for a chord of its own, set before any page loads.

  • Forgiving

    Close or clear something by mistake and Undo brings it back. No “are you sure?” dialogs.

Yours, and only yours

  1. No account

    Nothing to sign up for. Your tabs, history and settings stay on your computer.

  2. No telemetry

    Chord doesn't report what you do or where you go. Blocking and Safe Browsing check pages on your computer.

  3. No lock-in

    Open source and built in the open, on the same Chromium engine as the browsers you know. Bring your tabs and bookmarks in from the browser you use now, and take everything out in one file anyone can read.
